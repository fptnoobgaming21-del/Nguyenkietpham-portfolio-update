Moo'd

An organic milk brand, from carton to checkout

A self-initiated brand. A sour-candy box that unfolds into a game

A redesign of a Warheads candy package. After the last candy, a panel opens into a two-player tic-tac-toe board, giving people a reason to keep the box. I took it from sketches and vector layouts to three rounds of printed prototypes.

Bath fizzers packed like a tiny bathtub

A redesign for three peppermint bath fizzers. A calm red outside opens to an illustrated bathroom scene, with each fizzer in its own spot in a bathtub-shaped tray. Class critique pushed the lid and interior further; I printed and assembled three versions.

Album packaging and illustration, 2026

Chói (Shine)

An album redesigned in the language of Ukiyo-e

The first album by Ánh Sáng AZA, rebuilt as a full vinyl packaging system: two sleeve directions, back covers with the song list, Side A and Side B labels, and printed mockups. Waves, blossoms, vertical text panels and seal marks translate Edo-period woodblock prints into a calm, modern sleeve.

About me

I grew up in Ho Chi Minh City and now live in Minneapolis, where I'm studying for a BFA in Graphic Design with a minor in UX Design at the University of Minnesota, class of 2028.

I like projects where the hard part is making something clear: a dense research topic, a brand that has to feel friendly, a web page that has to work for everyone. Alongside school I've designed for the College of Liberal Arts, maintained Medical School web pages and written research news for the Department of Psychology. Vietnamese is my first language and English my second.
